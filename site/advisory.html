Advisory · Florida & New York

Advice at every stage of the ownership cycle.

We offer an in-depth understanding of dynamic business environments and the ability to respond to market conditions that can affect a single property or an entire portfolio. These are the services we provide, from the first look at a site to the day you sell.

01 Feasibility analysis

A thorough, data-driven study done before you buy land, build a new development or repurpose a property, so you know whether the project makes sense before money is committed.

02 Detailed investment modeling

We build a detailed financial model (real estate financial modeling, or REFM) that projects a property’s or portfolio’s performance, cash flow and risk over time. It shows equity investors, developers and lenders whether a deal meets their target returns before they commit capital.

03 Ownership structure alternatives

A property doesn’t have to be held in one person’s name. It can be bought, financed, managed and held through other legal and operating structures. We walk you through the alternatives and how each fits your goals, working alongside your attorney and tax advisor.

04 Acquisition and disposition strategies

Buying and selling are the two bookends of every investment. We plan how and when you enter and exit the market, whether the goal is to build wealth or to sharpen a portfolio.

05 Market research and due diligence

Two investigations that work together: one of the market, one of the property. Buyers, investors and lenders rely on them before closing to manage risk and avoid costly surprises.

How Rafael leads due diligence →

06 Debt and equity structuring

Combining layers of borrowed money (debt) and investor capital (equity) to finance the purchase, development or operation of a property.

07 Financing evaluation and negotiation

We compare the financing available, such as mortgages, loans and seller financing, and negotiate the terms that matter: interest rate, down payment, loan contingencies and closing concessions, so the purchase is feasible and affordable.

08 Property management

Running a property day to day: leasing, rent collection, tenant relations, maintenance, budgeting and reporting to owners.

09 Project management

Overseeing a development or renovation from start to finish, coordinating contractors, schedules, budgets and quality so the project is delivered on time and on budget.

10 Senior housing

Communities built for older adults, from independent living to memory care. As an investment, senior housing is driven by demographics and combines real estate with operating services.

Rafael’s healthcare and senior housing experience →

11 Assisted living

Senior housing where residents get help with daily activities such as bathing, medication and meals while keeping some independence. These properties are usually licensed and run as operating businesses; in Florida, assisted living facilities are licensed by the Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA).

Rafael’s healthcare and senior housing experience →

12 Business brokerage

Representing buyers or sellers of an operating business, often together with its real estate: valuation, marketing, finding qualified buyers and negotiating the deal.

13 Mergers & acquisitions

Combining or buying companies or real estate portfolios: valuation, due diligence, deal structure, and bringing the assets or operations together afterwards.

14 Design and zoning services

Coordinating architectural design with local land-use rules, including rezonings, variances, site plan approvals and permits, so a project can legally move forward.

15 Site selection

Finding and evaluating locations for a planned use, weighing demographics, access, visibility, zoning, infrastructure and cost.
