Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Continental Investments International Network LLC, doing business as Continental Investments International Network Co. (“we”, “us”), collects, uses and protects information when you visit this website or contact us through it. It applies to all pages of this website, including the pages for our Orlando, Florida and New York, New York offices.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use the contact form on this website, we collect:

  • your name and email address;
  • your phone number, if you choose to provide it;
  • what you’re interested in, and which office you’d like to talk to; and
  • anything you write in your message.

We also keep the information you share when you call or email us.

Information collected automatically. Our own pages do not use cookies, analytics tools or advertising trackers. Like most websites, our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time of the visit, to operate and secure the site.

Listings provided by Crexi. If you choose to show our commercial listings on this website, they are loaded from Crexi (Commercial Real Estate Exchange, Inc.). Nothing is loaded from Crexi until you press that button. When the listings viewer loads, Crexi may set cookies and collect information about your visit, such as your IP address and how you use the listings, under Crexi’s own privacy policy (opens in a new tab). We do not receive that information.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and connect you with the right agent.
  • To provide the real estate or consulting services you ask for.
  • To keep records of our communications, as required for our business and by law.
  • To protect the website and our clients against spam, fraud and misuse.

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • with our agents and staff who handle your inquiry;
  • with service providers that help us run this website, such as Netlify, which hosts the site and receives contact-form messages so they can be forwarded to us, and only as needed to provide that service; and
  • when required by law, or to protect our rights, our clients or others.

Links to other websites

Our website links to sites run by other companies, such as our listings on Crexi, residential listing sites and Google Maps. Their own privacy policies apply when you use those sites.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry messages for as long as we need them to respond, to provide our services and to meet our business and legal record-keeping obligations. After that, we delete them.

How we protect information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect the information you send us, including an encrypted (HTTPS) connection for this website, and we limit access to the people who need it to respond to you. No method of sending or storing information online is completely secure, so please don’t send sensitive details, such as bank or Social Security numbers, through the contact form.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us, subject to any records we are required to keep. Contact us using the details below. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

We do not send marketing email or text messages to people who contact us through this website unless they ask us to.

Children’s privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.
