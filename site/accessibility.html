Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed: October 14, 2026

Our commitment

Continental Investments International Network LLC wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we keep accessibility in mind as we update the site.

What we have done

  • The site can be used with a keyboard alone, and a “Skip to main content” link is available at the top of each page.
  • Links, buttons and form fields show a clear outline when they are selected with the keyboard.
  • Text and background colors have been checked for readable contrast.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and required fields are marked.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions.
  • Pages adjust to phones, tablets and computers, and still work when text is enlarged.
  • The home page uses animation and a 3D scene of New York and Florida. The scene is decorative: everything it shows is also written as regular text on the page, and screen readers skip it. If your device is set to reduce motion, the decorative motion is switched off. If your browser can't show 3D graphics, a still image is shown instead and every part of the page still works.
  • Pages are checked with an automated accessibility testing tool.

Known limitations

Some links take you to websites run by other companies, such as our listings on Crexi and residential listing sites. The optional listings viewer in the Listings section of the home page is also provided by Crexi. We don’t control those sites or that section, and they may not meet the same standards. If you have trouble using them, contact us and we will send you the property information directly.

Tell us about a problem

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or you need information in a different format, please contact us. Let us know which page and what you were trying to do, and we will work with you to provide the information you need.
