Rafael A. Gonzalez leads Continental Investments International Network Co. as broker and CEO. He is a licensed real estate broker in both Florida and New York and brings more than five decades of leadership across hospital operations, real estate development and institutional management.

His work spans commercial, institutional, land and faith-based property, with particular depth in assisted living, senior housing and medical-use real estate. He pairs an operator’s discipline with a developer’s eye to find, evaluate and close complex transactions.

03 Due diligence

Before a client commits, Rafael leads a full review of the property so the decision rests on facts rather than assumptions. On each transaction that covers:

  • Site analysis: access, layout, utilities and how the site can be used
  • Zoning: what is allowed today and the path to the approvals a project needs
  • Environmental screening: known conditions and risks on and around the site
  • Title research: ownership, liens and restrictions
  • Market valuation: value, demand and feasibility

For healthcare and senior housing projects, the review also covers state licensing, AHCA requirements and market feasibility for Florida projects.
