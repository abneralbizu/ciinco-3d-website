Local roots. Real experience. Relationships that last.

For Liza Figueroa, Central Florida isn’t just where she works. It’s home.

Liza grew up in Seminole County, went to school here, and has lived in the Oviedo and Winter Springs area since 1990. She has watched these neighborhoods grow and change, and she knows them the way only a longtime resident can: the streets, the schools and the people who give each community its character.

A licensed REALTOR® since 2014, Liza brings more than ten years of real estate experience and a background in construction, project management and real estate investment. That background helps her look past the listing photos, at how a home is built, what work it may need and how it could perform as an investment. Whether you’re buying your first home, selling a longtime family home, relocating or investing, you get practical advice you can act on.

What matters most to Liza is the people behind every transaction. Buying or selling a home is one of life’s biggest decisions, and she believes you deserve someone who listens, keeps you informed at every step and genuinely cares about your goals. Her friendly, hands-on approach keeps the process as smooth and stress-free as possible, and many of her client relationships last long after closing.

Fluent in English and Spanish, Liza serves buyers, sellers and investors in Seminole County, Orange County and the surrounding Central Florida communities.

Whether you’re buying your first home, making your next move or simply exploring your options, Liza would love to help you feel right at home.
